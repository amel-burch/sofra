Burek

Flaky pastry rolled with seasoned minced beef and onion, baked in a round tray.

Amra Hadžić 30 min prep · 45 min cook 6 servings medium 4.9 (18)
Instructions
  1. Mix flour, water and salt into a soft dough. Divide into two balls and rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Fry the onion lightly, add the minced beef, salt and pepper.
  3. Stretch the dough thin over a floured tablecloth and brush with oil.
  4. Spread the filling along the edge, roll into a long tube and coil it in a round tray.
  5. Bake at 220 °C for 40-45 minutes until golden. Rest 10 minutes under a towel.
